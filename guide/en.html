Online Lamp Lighting Registration
How to Register

Register on your phone or computer, then pay at the temple
Registration website: nytht.vercel.app
Press "EN" at the top left for English, and "Larger text" at the top right if the text is too small.
Start registering
Before you start, please have ready:
  • The contact person's name, phone number and address
  • For each person: name, gender and date of birth (Western or lunar calendar; the birth hour is optional)
  • Which lamps each person would like: Lamp of Blessing, Lamp of Wenchang, Lamp of Annual Taisui, Lamp of Wealth and Fortune ($120 each; you may choose more than one)
Each form is for one household (family members at the same address). Relatives at other addresses should fill in their own form.

How to register

1Tap “Register for lamps”

On the home page, in the Lamp Lighting box, tap Register for lamps.

It shows this year's lamp lighting and the price per lamp.

2Contact person

Enter the contact person's name, phone number and address. The temple uses these to reach you.

Then tap Next.

The bar at the top shows which step you are on (3 steps in all).

3Each person lighting a lamp

  • Full name: Chinese name if you have one, otherwise English. If the first person is the contact person, tap Same as contact.
  • Gender: tap Male or Female.
  • Date of birth: choose Western calendar or Lunar calendar, then the year, month and day. For a lunar leap month, tick Leap month. The birth hour can be left blank.
  • The zodiac sign and age appear automatically — please check they are right.
  • If the sign is advised to light the Taisui lamp this year, a reminder appears; tap Add Lamp of Annual Taisui to tick it.
  • Lamps: tick one or more lamps.

4More family members?

Tap + Add another person and fill in Person 2 the same way. Tap Remove to delete a person added by mistake.

When everyone is done, tap Next.

If something is missing, the box turns red with a note telling you what to fill in.

5Review and submit

You will see everything you entered, the number of each lamp and the total.

To change something, tap Edit next to it.

You can leave a message for the temple under Notes.

When everything is correct, tap Submit.

6Your registration form

After submitting, your registration form appears, showing:

  • Your registration number (e.g. L261108-003) — please write it down
  • Each person's lamp numbers (e.g. Lamp of Blessing G010104)
  • The amount due

If a lamp says Waitlist, that lamp is full; the temple will arrange it and contact you.

7Print or save it

Tap Print form and wait a few seconds. A small window appears:

  • Print: print it now. On iPhone, iPad and Android, a share menu opens first — choose Print in that menu.
  • Save as PDF: save it on your phone or computer to print later.

No printer? No problem — take a screenshot or write down your registration number. Show it at the temple and a volunteer will print it for you.

8Pay at the temple

Bring your form (or your phone screen) to the temple and pay by cash or check.

A volunteer will sign the form and cut off the receipt at the bottom for you.

Please keep the receipt. From the 9th day of Lunar New Year (the date is printed on the receipt), use your registration number to collect your lamp cards. If you lose it, just give your phone number and we can look it up.

Not sure which lamps to light?

The home page has a card called Which lamps should I light?

  • At the top it lists the zodiac signs advised to light the Taisui lamp this year. Tap What is the Taisui lamp? to read more.
  • Choose Western or Lunar calendar, enter a date of birth, and tick what you would like to pray for (peace and health, studies, work and prosperity).
  • It will suggest which lamps to light and why.
  • Tap Start lamp registration — the birthday and the suggested lamps are filled in for you.

These are only suggestions; choose whatever feels right. You can check for each family member.

Looking up, changing or adding

On the registration website, tap Look up / change my registration, enter your registration number and the phone number you registered with, then tap Look up.

  • You can see your lamp numbers again and print your form again.
  • Before paying, tap Make changes to edit your registration.
  • After paying, to add lamps or people, tap Add more lamps (new form). Your contact details are filled in; just add the new people and lamps. You will get a new registration number and pay for it at the temple.

If you registered on the same phone, My registration forms appears at the top of the home page — tap Open.

Common questions

I forgot my registration number / lost my receipt.
On the look-up page, tap Forgot my registration number. Enter the phone number you registered with and the name of anyone on the form, written the same way as when you registered. When collecting lamp cards at the temple, you can also just give your phone number.
I had to stop halfway. Is my form lost?
No. It is saved on this phone or computer. Tap Register for lamps again to continue, or Start over to begin again.
It says there is a network problem when I submit.
Your form has not been sent yet, and nothing you entered is lost. Wait a moment and tap Submit again — it will not register twice.
Can I register another household too?
Each form is for one address. When you finish one, tap Fill in another form for the next household.
