Lamp Registration Admin
Volunteer Manual

Finding registrations, taking payments (cash, check), lamp cards, reports
Admin address: nytht.vercel.app/admin
Works on phones, tablets and computers. Tap “Larger text” if the text is too small, or “中文” for Chinese.
Go to the admin
Everyday work:
  • Find the registration: tap “Scan” and scan the devotee's form, or type a phone number, registration number or name.
  • Take payment: cash or check, tap “Confirm”. Sign the paper form and cut off the receipt for the devotee.
  • End of shift: “Reports” → “My collections”, and check it against the cash and checks you hold.
From the 9th day of Lunar New Year (Feb 14, 2027), hand out lamp cards: find the registration → check the lamp numbers → give the cards → tap “Give lamp card to devotee”.

1. Logging in

1Tap your name

The screen lists everyone's names. Tap your own.

2Enter your PIN

Enter your PIN (4–8 digits) and tap Log in.

  • The first time you log in, you'll be asked to change it to a PIN only you know.
  • After 8 wrong tries in a row it locks for 15 minutes. If it's urgent, ask the temple head or the website admin to reset your PIN.

After logging in, the top bar has: 中文 (switch to Chinese), Larger text, your name, Reports, Records, Settings and Log out.

On the temple's shared computer or tablet, please tap “Log out” when you're done.

2. Finding a devotee's registration

Type any of these in the search box and the registration appears below:

  • Phone number: the last few digits are enough
  • Registration number: e.g. L261105-003
  • Name: the contact person or anyone lighting a lamp
  • Lamp number: e.g. G010104

The buttons below show only Unpaid / Paid / Cancelled / Card not collected.

“Scan” is fastest

Tap Scan to the right of the search box and point it at the QR code on the form or the receipt. The registration opens right away.

  • The first time, it asks to use the camera — tap “Allow”.
  • Scan from inside the admin, not with your phone's Camera app. The Camera app opens the devotee look-up page, not the admin.
  • If the camera won't open, just type the registration number from the receipt.

Each registration is a card showing:

  • Registration number and time
  • Status: red “Unpaid”, green “Paid” or “Cancelled”
  • Contact person, phone, and each person's lamps and lamp numbers
  • At the bottom, the amount due and the payment button

Small tags: “Edited by devotee” means the devotee changed the form; “Waitlisted” means a lamp has no number yet.

3. Taking cash

1Check the amount

Compare the registration number and amount due on the devotee's form with the card. The devotee may have changed the form, so go by the amount on screen.

2Tap “Receive payment: mark paid”

Choose Cash. It asks you to confirm the cash amount; tap Confirm. The system records you as the person who took it.

3Sign the paper form, cut off the receipt

  • At the bottom of the form, write the amount received, tick cash or check, sign and date. Do the same on the receipt.
  • Cut along the dotted line and give the bottom receipt to the devotee. Keep the top part at the temple.
  • If the devotee didn't bring the form, tap “Print form” on the card to print it.

4. Taking a check

Tap the payment button, choose Check, and fill in four boxes:

  • Name on check: the name printed at the top left, typed exactly as printed. Not the “Pay to the order of” line — that's the temple.
  • Check no.: the number at the top right.
  • Check amount: the amount of the whole check.
  • Amount used: how much of the check pays this registration. It's filled in for you.

Below it shows the amount due and how much the check covers. If it's right, tap Confirm.

Check isn't enough?

A red line shows “+ cash $… (collect in cash)”. Collect the rest in cash, then confirm. It's recorded as “check + cash”. A short check can't be marked paid on its own.

One check for several forms?

When you take the next form, choose Check: your recent check and its balance appear. Tap Use this. You can also type the same number and name yourself.

Check has money left and won't be used again?

For example, the extra is a donation: tick “Check won't be used again — record what the balance is for” and write the purpose, e.g. “Donation”.

If it will pay for another form, don't tick it yet. Finance can also add this later in the reports.

A check someone else took

You can only see checks you took today. If the number and name match a check someone else took, a receipt code box appears:

  • Ask the devotee for the receipt of a form that check already paid, and type the 4-digit receipt code on it. If it's right, you can use the balance.
  • No receipt, or locked after 3 wrong codes? Ask finance or the temple head.
  • If the name is similar but not the same, the screen asks you to check the spelling.

5. Made a mistake?

  • Edit payment: the payment method or check details are wrong (e.g. a typo in the number, or it was actually a check). The date and who took it stay the same.
  • Change back to unpaid: no money was actually received, or it's a refund.

Both are at the bottom of a paid card. You can only change payments you took; for others, ask finance or the temple head. Every change is recorded.

6. Lamp cards (from the 9th day of Lunar New Year)

Lamp cards are handed out from the 9th day of Lunar New Year (Feb 14, 2027). Before that, cards only show a gray “Cards from Feb 14” tag.

  1. Tap “Scan” and scan the receipt, or type the registration number or phone.
  2. Check the lamp numbers and give the cards to the devotee.
  3. Tap the brown Give lamp card to devotee → “Confirm”.

The card then shows “Card collected”, the date and who gave it — so if family members come separately, you can see at a glance.

  • Only paid registrations can get cards. Gave it by mistake? Tap “Change back to card not collected”.
  • Use the “Card not collected” filter to see who has paid but not yet collected.

7. Cancelling, waitlist, add-ons

  • Cancel registration: the devotee changed their mind. Tap “Cancel registration” → “Confirm”; the lamp spots are freed. Only unpaid registrations can be cancelled. Cancelled by mistake? Tap “Restore registration”.
  • Waitlist: when lamps are full, it says “Waitlist”. When someone cancels and a spot opens, tap “Assign waitlisted lamps”, then call the devotee with the new lamp number.
  • Add-ons: a devotee who has paid and wants more lamps fills in a new form with a new number, marked “Add-on to L…”. Payment and cards are handled separately.

8. Records

Tap “Records” at the top to see the payments, payment edits, lamp cards and cancellations you handled, newest first. Tap a registration number to open it.

Records can't be edited or deleted. The temple head, deputy and finance can see everyone's records.

9. Reports (end-of-shift check)

Tap “Reports” at the top and choose one:

  • My collections: every payment you took, cash and checks listed separately. At the end of your shift, tap “Today”, check it against the cash and checks you hold, then hand them to finance.
  • Lamp register: people and lamp numbers by lamp type, for making lamp cards and setting up lamps.
  • Annual Tai Sui table: which signs are affected by Tai Sui this year and the remedies — can be printed and posted.

Then tap Print. Tap “Back to registrations” when done.

10. Changing your PIN

Tap “Settings”. Under “My PIN”, enter your current PIN and the new PIN (twice), then tap “Update my PIN”.

Forgot your PIN? Ask the temple head or the website admin to reset it.

11. The temple's shared tablet

A tablet for devotees to register at the temple must be set as a shared device, so their information isn't left on it:

  • Turn on: on the tablet, open nytht.vercel.app and tap the small “Set as shared device” link at the very bottom → “OK”.
  • The bottom then says “Shared device: nothing is kept on this tablet”.
  • After a devotee submits, ask them to print the form or take a photo with their own phone, then tap “Next person”. It also clears itself after a few minutes of no use.
  • Turn off: on the tablet, open nytht.vercel.app/?kiosk=0 (there's no turn-off button on the tablet, so devotees can't tap it by accident).

12. Roles and permissions

RoleWhat they can do
VolunteerFind registrations, take payments, hand out lamp cards, cancel. Sees only their own payments and checks.
FinanceEverything a volunteer can, plus see all accounts, handle checks others took, and change payments others took.
Website adminEverything a volunteer can, plus open/pause registration and manage volunteer accounts. Can't see others' accounts.
DeputySame as the temple head, but can't change the temple head's account. Only one.
Temple headEverything. Only one.

13. Finance, temple head: Payment summary and checks

“Reports” → “Payment summary”: amount due, amount paid, actual cash and checks received, and how much each volunteer took.

  • Other income: money from checks beyond the registration fees.
  • Checks received: one group per check, listing the forms it paid.
  • When a check has unallocated money, tap “Set purpose” and write what it's for (e.g. donation). To change it, tap “Edit”, then Update or Delete.

14. Temple head, deputy, website admin: Settings

Open / pause registration, lamp year

  • Open: devotees can register. Paused: shown but can't be registered for. Hidden (ceremonies): not shown on the signup page.
  • Lamp year: ages are counted for this year. When the year changes, lamp numbers start over; earlier records are kept.
  • Signs advised to light the Tai Sui lamp this year: tick them following the list announced by the Songshan Tsu Hui Temple.
  • Always tap Save registration settings when done.

Accounts

  • Add: enter a name, choose a role, set a PIN and tap “Add”. Tell them the PIN; they'll set their own at first login.
  • Edit: change the name or role, or Reset PIN (if forgotten or locked).
  • Disable: for people who no longer help — their records are kept. Accounts created by mistake can be deleted.
  • Assigning finance or website admin: temple head or deputy. Assigning temple head or deputy: temple head only.
  • When the temple head makes someone else temple head, that's a handover; the old temple head becomes a volunteer.

Changes to accounts, PINs and registration settings are kept on the “Records” page.
